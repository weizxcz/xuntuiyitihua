BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create Left Mounting Bracket  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.020×0.150×0.005 m and name it bracket_L.
Rotate bracket_L so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move bracket_L to pos=(-0.150, 0.075, -0.025).
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.006 m and length 0.010 m and name it drill_bit_L.
Rotate drill_bit_L so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move drill_bit_L to pos=(-0.150, 0.025, -0.025).
Boolean-subtract drill_bit_L from bracket_L; discard drill_bit_L.
Stage 1 complete.
