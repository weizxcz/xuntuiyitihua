BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create main scarf body  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.25×1.8×0.002 m and name it scarf_main.
Rotate scarf_main so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor scarf_main to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).

---  SECTION 2 – Create left fringe cluster  ---
Create Empty helper (cube display) and name it fringe_L.
Rotate fringe_L so its axis aligns +Z (world).
Move fringe_L to (0,0,0).
Scale fringe_L to match overall size (AUTO → 0.002 m inferred height).
Align fringe_L.+Z_face to scarf_main.+Z_face.
Align fringe_L to scarf_main.left_face.center.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).

For i in 0…19:
  Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.001 m and length 0.15 m and name it fringe_L_thread_i.
  Rotate fringe_L_thread_i so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
  Anchor fringe_L_thread_i to fringe_L.
  Then offset(dx=-0.12 + i*0.0127, dy=0, dz=0).

---  SECTION 3 – Create right fringe cluster  ---
Create Empty helper (cube display) and name it fringe_R.
Rotate fringe_R so its axis aligns +Z (world).
Move fringe_R to (0,0,0).
Scale fringe_R to match overall size (AUTO → 0.002 m inferred height).
Align fringe_R.+Z_face to scarf_main.+Z_face.
Align fringe_R to scarf_main.right_face.center.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).

For i in 0…19:
  Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.001 m and length 0.15 m and name it fringe_R_thread_i.
  Rotate fringe_R_thread_i so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
  Anchor fringe_R_thread_i to fringe_R.
  Then offset(dx=-0.12 + i*0.0127, dy=0, dz=0).

---  SECTION 4 – Assemble final scarf body  ---
Boolean-union scarf_main, fringe_L, and fringe_R to create scarf_body.
Align scarf_body.center to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).

Assembly guideline satisfied.
