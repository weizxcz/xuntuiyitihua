BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material mat_steel_handle; diffuse_color = (0.2, 0.2, 0.2, 1.0).
Define material mat_steel_jaw; diffuse_color = (0.6, 0.6, 0.6, 1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create Standard Pliers Jaw (pliers_jaw)  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.020 m and length 0.150 m and name it jaw_left.
Rotate jaw_left so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align jaw_left.right_face to pliers_jaw.center.
Then move by offset(0, 0, 0).

Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.020 m and length 0.150 m and name it jaw_right.
Rotate jaw_right so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align jaw_right.right_face to jaw_left.left_face.
Then move by offset(0, 0, 0).

Boolean-union jaw_left and jaw_right; keep jaw_left.
Rename jaw_left to pliers_jaw.

---  SECTION 2 – Create Standard Pliers Handle (pliers_handle)  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.025 m and length 0.100 m and name it pliers_handle.
Rotate pliers_handle so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align pliers_handle.center to pliers_main.center.
Then move by offset(0, 0, 0).

---  SECTION 3 – Assemble Standard Pliers Main (pliers_main)  ---
Boolean-union pliers_jaw and pliers_handle; keep pliers_jaw.
Rename pliers_jaw to pliers_main.

---  SECTION 4 – Create Standard Pliers Pivot (pliers_pivot)  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.005 m and length 0.030 m and name it pliers_pivot.
Rotate pliers_pivot so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align pliers_pivot.center to pliers_main.pivot_hole.
Then move by offset(0, 0, 0).

Boolean-union pliers_main and pliers_pivot; keep pliers_main.
Rename pliers_main to pliers_union.

---  SECTION 5 – Create Needle-Nose Pliers Jaw (needle_jaw)  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.005 m and length 0.200 m and name it needle_tip_left.
Rotate needle_tip_left so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align needle_tip_left.right_face to needle_jaw.center.
Then move by offset(0, 0, 0).

Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.005 m and length 0.200 m and name it needle_tip_right.
Rotate needle_tip_right so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align needle_tip_right.right_face to needle_tip_left.left_face.
Then move by offset(0, 0, 0).

Boolean-union needle_tip_left and needle_tip_right; keep needle_tip_left.
Rename needle_tip_left to needle_jaw.

---  SECTION 6 – Create Needle-Nose Pliers Handle (needle_handle)  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.020 m and length 0.080 m and name it needle_handle.
Rotate needle_handle so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align needle_handle.center to needle_pliers_main.center.
Then move by offset(0, 0, 0).

---  SECTION 7 – Assemble Needle-Nose Pliers Main (needle_pliers_main)  ---
Boolean-union needle_jaw and needle_handle; keep needle_jaw.
Rename needle_jaw to needle_pliers_main.

---  SECTION 8 – Create Needle-Nose Pliers Pivot (needle_pliers_pivot)  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.004 m and length 0.025 m and name it needle_pliers_pivot.
Rotate needle_pliers_pivot so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align needle_pliers_pivot.center to needle_pliers_main.pivot_hole.
Then move by offset(0, 0, 0).

Boolean-union needle_pliers_main and needle_pliers_pivot; keep needle_pliers_main.
Rename needle_pliers_main to needle_pliers_union.

---  SECTION 9 – Final Assembly of Composite Pliers  ---
Align needle_pliers_union.center to (0,0,0.05).
Then move by offset(0, 0, 0).

Boolean-union pliers_union and needle_pliers_union; keep pliers_union.
Rename pliers_union to pliers_final.

Stage 9 complete.
All stages complete.
