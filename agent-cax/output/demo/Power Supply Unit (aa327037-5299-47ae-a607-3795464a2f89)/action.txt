---  SECTION 1 – Create PSU Chassis Base  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.150×0.086×0.015 m and name it psu_chassis.
Rotate psu_chassis so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move psu_chassis to (0,0,0).

---  SECTION 2 – Create PSU Case  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.150×0.086×0.015 m and name it psu_case.
Rotate psu_case so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align psu_case.center to psu_chassis.center.
Boolean-subtract psu_case from psu_chassis; keep psu_chassis. // Creates hollow interior
Assembly guideline satisfied.
Stage 2 complete.
