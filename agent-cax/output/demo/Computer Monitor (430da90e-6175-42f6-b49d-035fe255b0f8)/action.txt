BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create screen panel  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.600×0.340×0.005 m and name it screen_panel.
Rotate screen_panel so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move screen_panel to pos=(0, 0.170, 0).
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create bezel frame  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.620×0.360×0.008 m and name it bezel_frame.
Rotate bezel_frame so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align bezel_frame.center to screen_panel.center.
Align bezel_frame.front_face to screen_panel.front_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.002).
Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Create stand base  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.150 m and height 0.010 m and name it stand_base.

Stage 3 complete.

---  SECTION 4 – Create stand pillar  ---

Then move by offset(0,0,0.005).
Stage 4 complete.

---  SECTION 5 – Create stand connector  ---
