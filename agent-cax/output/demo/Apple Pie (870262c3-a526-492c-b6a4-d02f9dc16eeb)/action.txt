BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material crust_dough; diffuse_color = (0.96, 0.84, 0.62, 1.0).
Define material fill_apple; diffuse_color = (0.85, 0.15, 0.10, 0.9).
Define material glaze_eggs; diffuse_color = (0.95, 0.85, 0.50, 0.6).
Define material sugar_crystal; diffuse_color = (1.0, 1.0, 1.0, 0.8).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create pie base (crust_base)  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.100 m and height 0.015 m and name it crust_base.
Rotate crust_base so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align crust_base.+Z_face to table.+Z_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Apply material crust_dough to crust_base.

---  SECTION 2 – Create filling  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.090 m and height 0.020 m and name it filling.
Rotate filling so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align filling.+Z_face to crust_base.+Z_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.015).
Apply material fill_apple to filling.

---  SECTION 3 – Create crust top  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.100 m and height 0.005 m and name it crust_top.
Rotate crust_top so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align crust_top.+Z_face to crust_base.+Z_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.035).
Apply material crust_dough to crust_top.

---  SECTION 4 – Create glaze  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.095 m and height 0.001 m and name it glaze.
Rotate glaze so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align glaze.+Z_face to crust_top.+Z_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.040).
Apply material glaze_eggs to glaze.

---  SECTION 5 – Create sugar crystals  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.095 m and height 0.0005 m and name it sugar.
Rotate sugar so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align sugar.+Z_face to crust_top.+Z_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.041).
Apply material sugar_crystal to sugar.

Stage 5 complete.
All stages complete.
