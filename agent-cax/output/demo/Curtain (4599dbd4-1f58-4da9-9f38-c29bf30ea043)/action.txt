BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create rod_main  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.025 m and length 1.8 m and name it rod_main.
Rotate rod_main so its +Z axis aligns +X (world).

---  SECTION 2 – Create finial_L  ---
