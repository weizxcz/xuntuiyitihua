BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material ceramic_white; diffuse_color = (0.95,0.95,0.95,1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create base_disc  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.203 m and height 0.025 m and name it base_disc.
Rotate base_disc so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor base_disc to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).

---  SECTION 2 – Create outer_wall  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.203 m and height 0.076 m and name it outer_wall.
Rotate outer_wall so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor outer_wall to base_disc.+Z_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.025).

---  SECTION 3 – Create inner_wall  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.178 m and height 0.051 m and name it inner_wall.
Rotate inner_wall so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor inner_wall to base_disc.+Z_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.025).

---  SECTION 4 – Assemble Bowl  ---
Boolean-union outer_wall and inner_wall to base_disc; keep base_disc.
Assembly guideline satisfied.
Stage 4 complete.

All stages complete.
