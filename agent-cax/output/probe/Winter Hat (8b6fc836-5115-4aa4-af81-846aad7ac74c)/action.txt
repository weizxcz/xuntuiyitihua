BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material fabric_main; diffuse_color = (0.85,0.15,0.15,1.0).
Define material fur_pom; diffuse_color = (0.95,0.95,0.90,1.0).
Define material fur_trim; diffuse_color = (0.95,0.95,0.90,1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create hat body  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.110 m and height 0.200 m and name it hat_body.
Rotate hat_body so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor hat_body to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create ear flaps  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.040 m and height 0.150 m and name it flap_L.
Rotate flap_L so its +Z axis aligns +Y (world).
Align flap_L.center to hat_body.side_at(180°).
Then move by offset(0,0,-0.050).

Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.040 m and height 0.150 m and name it flap_R.
Rotate flap_R so its +Z axis aligns +Y (world).
Align flap_R.center to hat_body.side_at(0°).
Then move by offset(0,0,-0.050).
Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Create pom-pom and trim ring  ---
Create primitive sphere with radius 0.050 m and name it pom_pom.
Rotate pom_pom so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align pom_pom.bottom to hat_body.top.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.020).

Create primitive torus with major radius 0.110 m and minor radius 0.015 m and name it trim_ring.
Rotate trim_ring so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align trim_ring.center to hat_body.top.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.001).
Stage 3 complete.

All stages complete.
