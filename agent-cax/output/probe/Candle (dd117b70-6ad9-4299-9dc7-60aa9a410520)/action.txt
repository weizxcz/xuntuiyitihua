BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material candle_wax; diffuse_color = (1.00, 1.00, 0.94, 1).
Define material wick_cotton; diffuse_color = (0.20, 0.16, 0.12, 1).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create candle body  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.040 m and height 0.150 m and name it candle_body.
Rotate candle_body so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align candle_body.center to candle_root.center.
Align candle_body.bottom_face to candle_root.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create wick  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.002 m and height 0.020 m and name it wick.
Rotate wick so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align wick.center to candle_body.center.
Align wick.bottom_face to candle_body.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.002).
Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Assemble root  ---
Align candle_root.center to candle_body.center.
Align candle_root.bottom_face to candle_body.bottom_face.
Align wick.center to candle_body.center.
Align wick.bottom_face to candle_body.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.002).
Assembly guideline satisfied.
Stage 3 complete.

All stages complete.
