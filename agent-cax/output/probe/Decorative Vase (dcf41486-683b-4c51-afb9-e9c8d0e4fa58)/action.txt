BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material ceramic_white; diffuse_color = (0.95,0.95,0.95,1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create vase_base  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.110 m and height 0.020 m and name it vase_base.
Rotate vase_base so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor vase_base to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.010).

---  SECTION 2 – Create vase_body  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.100 m and height 0.300 m and name it vase_body.
Rotate vase_body so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor vase_body to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.150).

---  SECTION 3 – Create vase_rim  ---
Create primitive torus with major radius 0.070 m and minor radius 0.0025 m and name it vase_rim.
Rotate vase_rim so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor vase_rim to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.300).

---  SECTION 4 – Assemble Vase  ---
Boolean-union vase_body, vase_base, and vase_rim; keep vase_body.
Rename vase_body to vase.
Assembly guideline satisfied.
Stage 4 complete.

All stages complete.
