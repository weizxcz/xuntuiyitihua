BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material mat_cork; diffuse_color = (0.6, 0.5, 0.3, 1.0).
Define material mat_rubber; diffuse_color = (0.1, 0.1, 0.1, 1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create coaster base  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.102 m and height 0.005 m and name it coaster_base.
Rotate coaster_base so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor coaster_base to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Apply material mat_cork to coaster_base.
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create rubber ring  ---
Create primitive torus with major radius 0.051 m and minor radius 0.001 m and name it rubber_ring.
Rotate rubber_ring so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align rubber_ring.center to coaster_base.center.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.001).
Apply material mat_rubber to rubber_ring.
Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Create rubber center  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.050 m and height 0.002 m and name it rubber_center.
Rotate rubber_center so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align rubber_center.center to coaster_base.center.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.001).
Apply material mat_rubber to rubber_center.
Stage 3 complete.

All stages complete.
