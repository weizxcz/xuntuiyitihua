BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material mat_black; diffuse_color = (0.1,0.1,0.1,1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create outer ring  ---
Create primitive torus with Outer Radius 0.100 m, Tube Radius 0.010 m and name it outer_ring.
Rotate outer_ring so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align outer_ring.center to peace_symbol.center.
Align outer_ring.bottom_face to peace_symbol.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create vertical stem  ---
Create primitive cylinder with Ø0.020 m and height 0.200 m and name it vertical_stem.
Rotate vertical_stem so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align vertical_stem.center to peace_symbol.center.
Align vertical_stem.bottom_face to peace_symbol.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Create left diagonal  ---
Create primitive cylinder with Ø0.020 m and height 0.141 m and name it left_diagonal.
Rotate left_diagonal so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Rotate left_diagonal so its local +Z axis achieves orientation=axis:+Z (world) then apply rotation=axis:Z,135°.
Align left_diagonal.center to peace_symbol.center.
Align left_diagonal.bottom_face to peace_symbol.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 3 complete.

---  SECTION 4 – Create right diagonal  ---
Create primitive cylinder with Ø0.020 m and height 0.141 m and name it right_diagonal.
Rotate right_diagonal so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Rotate right_diagonal so its local +Z axis achieves orientation=axis:+Z (world) then apply rotation=axis:Z,45°.
Align right_diagonal.center to peace_symbol.center.
Align right_diagonal.bottom_face to peace_symbol.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 4 complete.

---  SECTION 5 – Assemble inner lines union  ---
Boolean-union vertical_stem, left_diagonal, and right_diagonal to create inner_lines.
Align inner_lines.center to peace_symbol.center.
Align inner_lines.bottom_face to peace_symbol.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 5 complete.

---  SECTION 6 – Assemble peace symbol union  ---
Boolean-union outer_ring and inner_lines to create peace_symbol.
Assembly guideline satisfied.
Stage 6 complete.

All stages complete.
