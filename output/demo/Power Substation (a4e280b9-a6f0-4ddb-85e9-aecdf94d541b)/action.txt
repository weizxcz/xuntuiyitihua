BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create Foundation Pad  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 20×15×0.3 m and name it site_pad.
Rotate site_pad so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor site_pad to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 1 complete.

Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Install Major Equipment  ---

Rotate transformer_T1 so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align transformer_T1.base to support_grid_A.top_face.

---  SECTION 4 – Install Electrical Distribution  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 5×0.1×0.1 m and name it busbar_main_H.
Rotate busbar_main_H so its +Z axis aligns +X (world).
Align busbar_main_H.center to Avg(transformer_T1.top_center, transformer_T2.top_center).
Then move by offset(0,0,1.5).

Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.1 and height 0.5 m and name it insulator_set_T1.
