---  SECTION 1 – Create base petal template (petal_0)  ---
Create primitive plane with size 0.04×0.06 m and name it petal_0.
Rotate petal_0 so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align petal_0.center to floral_symbol.center.
Align petal_0.bottom_face to floral_symbol.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Bevel petal_0 with radius 0.001 m, segments 4.
Boolean-union petal_0 with itself to ensure solid caps (extrude simulation via thickness).
