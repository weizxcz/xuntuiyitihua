---  SECTION 1 – Create Shell Body (Front & Back Panels)  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.3×0.6×0.002 m and name it shell_body_front.
Rotate shell_body_front so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor shell_body_front to world origin.
Move by offset(0,0,0).

Create primitive cuboid with size 0.3×0.6×0.002 m and name it shell_body_back.
Rotate shell_body_back so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor shell_body_back to shell_body_front.bottom_face.
Align shell_body_back.XY_face to shell_body_front.XY_face.
Move by offset(0,0.01,0).

Create primitive cuboid with size 0.6×1.2×0.02 m and name it shell_body_union.
Rotate shell_body_union so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor shell_body_union to world origin.
Move by offset(0,0,0).

Boolean-union shell_body_front and shell_body_back into shell_body_union; discard shell_body_front and shell_body_back.
Rename shell_body_union to shell_body.

Stage 1 complete.
