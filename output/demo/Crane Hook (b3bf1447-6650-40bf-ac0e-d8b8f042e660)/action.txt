BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material steel_hardened; diffuse_color = (0.3, 0.3, 0.35, 1.0).
Define material steel_spring; diffuse_color = (0.2, 0.2, 0.25, 1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create Hook Body (Shank, Throat, Tip)  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.04 m and length 0.3 m and name it shank.
Rotate shank so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move shank to (0, 0, 0.15).

Create primitive torus with major radius 0.06 m and minor radius 0.02 m and name it throat.
Rotate throat so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align throat.+Z_face to shank.+Z_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).

Create primitive cone with base radius 0.02 m and height 0.04 m and name it tip.
Rotate tip so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align tip.+Z_face to throat.+Z_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).

Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create Safety Latch Mechanism (Latch Arm, Tip, Spring, Pin)  ---
Create primitive box with size 0.01×0.01×0.08 m and name it latch_arm.
Rotate latch_arm so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align latch_arm.XY_center to throat.side_at(0°).
Then move by offset(0,0,0.02).

Create primitive sphere with radius 0.015 m and name it latch_tip.
Rotate latch_tip so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align latch_tip.XY_center to latch_arm.+Z_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.04).

Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.005 m and length 0.02 m and name it spring.
Rotate spring so its +Z axis aligns +X (world).
Align spring.XY_center to latch_arm.side_at(90°).
Then move by offset(0,0,0.04).

Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.002 m and length 0.02 m and name it pin.
Rotate pin so its +Z axis aligns +X (world).
Align pin.XY_center to latch_arm.side_at(90°).
Then move by offset(0,0,0.04).

Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Assemble Crane Hook  ---
Connect crane_hook_assembly to hook_body, safety_latch, spring, and pin.

All stages complete.
