BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material wood_dark; diffuse_color = (0.35, 0.20, 0.10, 1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create central vertical support  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.020×0.020×1.800 m and name it support_back.
Rotate support_back so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor support_back to world_origin(0,0,0).
Align support_back.bottom_face to world_origin(0,0,0).
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Apply material wood_dark to support_back.
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create bottom triangular shelf tier  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.800×0.800×0.025 m and name it tier_1.
Rotate tier_1 so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor tier_1 to support_back.
Align tier_1.center to Avg(support_back.left_face, support_back.right_face).
Align tier_1.bottom_face to support_back.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.025).
Apply material wood_dark to tier_1.
Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Create middle triangular shelf tier  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.600×0.600×0.025 m and name it tier_2.
Rotate tier_2 so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor tier_2 to support_back.
Align tier_2.center to Avg(support_back.left_face, support_back.right_face).
Align tier_2.bottom_face to support_back.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.600).
Apply material wood_dark to tier_2.
Stage 3 complete.

---  SECTION 4 – Create top triangular shelf tier  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.400×0.400×0.025 m and name it tier_3.
Rotate tier_3 so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor tier_3 to support_back.
Align tier_3.center to Avg(support_back.left_face, support_back.right_face).
Align tier_3.bottom_face to support_back.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,1.200).
Apply material wood_dark to tier_3.
Stage 4 complete.

All stages complete.
