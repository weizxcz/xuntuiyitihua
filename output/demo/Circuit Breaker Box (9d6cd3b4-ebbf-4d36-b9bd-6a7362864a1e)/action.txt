---  SECTION 1 – Create outer shell  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.300×0.200×0.150 m and name it outer_shell.
Rotate outer_shell so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move outer_shell to (0,0,0).

---  SECTION 2 – Create inner void (hollow)  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.280×0.180×0.140 m and name it inner_void.
Rotate inner_void so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move inner_void to (0,0,0).
Boolean-subtract inner_void from outer_shell; discard inner_void.

---  SECTION 3 – Create top mounting rail  ---
Create primitive cylinder with Ø0.010×0.280 m and name it mounting_rail_top.
Rotate mounting_rail_top so its +Z axis aligns +X (world).
Move mounting_rail_top to (0, 0.085, 0.070).

---  SECTION 4 – Create bottom mounting rail  ---
Create primitive cylinder with Ø0.010×0.280 m and name it mounting_rail_bottom.
Rotate mounting_rail_bottom so its +Z axis aligns +X (world).
Move mounting_rail_bottom to (0, -0.085, 0.070).

---  SECTION 5 – Assemble housing components  ---
Align(Z) mounting_rail_top.bottom_face to outer_shell.top_face.
Align(Z) mounting_rail_bottom.bottom_face to outer_shell.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.005).
Assembly guideline satisfied.
Stage 5 complete.
