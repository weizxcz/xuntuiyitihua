BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material ceramic_white; diffuse_color = (0.95,0.95,0.95,1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create vase_base  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.16 m and height 0.02 m and name it vase_base.
Rotate vase_base so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor vase_base to vase.center.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create vase_body  ---
Create primitive cylinder with base diameter 0.15 m, top diameter 0.05 m, and height 0.25 m and name it vase_body.
Rotate vase_body so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align vase_body.bottom_face to vase_base.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Create vase_lip  ---
Create primitive torus with major diameter 0.05 m and minor diameter 0.005 m and name it vase_lip.
Rotate vase_lip so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align vase_lip.center to vase_body.top_face.
Align vase_lip.bottom_face to vase_body.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.002).
Stage 3 complete.

All stages complete.
