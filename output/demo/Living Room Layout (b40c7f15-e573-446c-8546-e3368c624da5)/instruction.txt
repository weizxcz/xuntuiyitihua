Let's design a living room layout. Place a sectional sofa in an L-shape against one corner. In front of the sofa, add a rectangular coffee table. Position a TV stand with a flat-screen TV opposite the sofa. Add a floor lamp next to the sofa and a bookshelf against one wall. Place a rug under the coffee table, extending to the front legs of the sofa. Add a small plant on the coffee table and a larger plant in one corner of the room.
