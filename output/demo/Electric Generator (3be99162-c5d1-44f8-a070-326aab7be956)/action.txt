BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create housing  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.200 m and height 0.150 m and name it housing_cylinder.
Rotate housing_cylinder so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move housing_cylinder to (0,0,0).

Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.220 m and height 0.010 m and name it mounting_flange.
Rotate mounting_flange so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align mounting_flange.center to housing_cylinder.center.
Align mounting_flange.top_face to housing_cylinder.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,-0.005).
Boolean-union mounting_flange into housing_cylinder; discard mounting_flange.
Rename housing_cylinder to housing.

Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create stator_assembly  ---
Create primitive torus with outer radius 0.090 m, inner radius 0.070 m, and tube radius 0.015 m and name it stator_core.
Rotate stator_core so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move stator_core to (0,0,0).

Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.030 m and height 0.040 m and name it coil_former_0.
Rotate coil_former_0 so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move coil_former_0 to (0.080,0,0).

Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.020 m and height 0.035 m and name it copper_coil_0.
Rotate copper_coil_0 so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align copper_coil_0.center to coil_former_0.center.
Align copper_coil_0.bottom_face to coil_former_0.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.002).
Boolean-union copper_coil_0 into coil_former_0; discard copper_coil_0.
Rename coil_former_0 to coil_group_0.

For i in 0…3:
  Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.030 m and height 0.040 m and name it coil_former_i.
  Rotate coil_former_i so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
  Align coil_former_i.center to stator_core.center.
  Align coil_former_i.bottom_face to stator_core.bottom_face.
  Then position at polar(0° + i·90°; dr=0.080).

  Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.020 m and height 0.035 m and name it copper_coil_i.
  Rotate copper_coil_i so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
  Align copper_coil_i.center to coil_former_i.center.
  Align copper_coil_i.bottom_face to coil_former_i.bottom_face.
  Then move by offset(0,0,0.002).
  Boolean-union copper_coil_i into coil_former_i; discard copper_coil_i.
  Rename coil_former_i to coil_group_i.

Boolean-union coil_group_0 into stator_core; discard coil_group_0.
Boolean-union coil_group_1 into stator_core; discard coil_group_1.
Boolean-union coil_group_2 into stator_core; discard coil_group_2.
Boolean-union coil_group_3 into stator_core; discard coil_group_3.
Rename stator_core to stator_assembly.

Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Create rotor_assembly  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.020 m and height 0.160 m and name it rotor_shaft.
Rotate rotor_shaft so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move rotor_shaft to (0,0,0).

Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.060 m and height 0.040 m and name it rotor_core.
Rotate rotor_core so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align rotor_core.center to rotor_shaft.center.
Align rotor_core.bottom_face to rotor_shaft.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.001).
Boolean-union rotor_core into rotor_shaft; discard rotor_core.
Rename rotor_shaft to rotor_assembly.

Create primitive cube with size 0.015×0.015×0.010 m and name it magnet_block_0.
Rotate magnet_block_0 so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move magnet_block_0 to (0.030,0,0.020).

For i in 0…3:
  Create primitive cube with size 0.015×0.015×0.010 m and name it magnet_block_i.
  Rotate magnet_block_i so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
  Align magnet_block_i.center to rotor_core.center.
  Align magnet_block_i.bottom_face to rotor_core.top_face.
  Then position at polar(0° + i·90°; dr=0.030).

Boolean-union magnet_block_0 into rotor_assembly; discard magnet_block_0.
Boolean-union magnet_block_1 into rotor_assembly; discard magnet_block_1.
Boolean-union magnet_block_2 into rotor_assembly; discard magnet_block_2.
Boolean-union magnet_block_3 into rotor_assembly; discard magnet_block_3.

Stage 3 complete.

---  SECTION 4 – Final Assembly  ---
Align rotor_assembly.center to stator_assembly.center.
Align rotor_assembly.bottom_face to stator_assembly.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.005).
Boolean-union rotor_assembly into housing; discard rotor_assembly.
Boolean-union stator_assembly into housing; discard stator_assembly.
Rename housing to generator_assembly.

Assembly guideline satisfied.
All stages complete.
