---  SECTION 1 – Create case_body  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.400×0.300×0.150 m and name it case_body.
Rotate case_body so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor case_body to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.390×0.290×0.145 m and name it case_body_inner_cut.
Rotate case_body_inner_cut so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor case_body_inner_cut to case_body.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.0025).
Boolean-subtract case_body_inner_cut from case_body; discard case_body_inner_cut.
Bevel case_body.edges with radius 0.020 m, segments 8.
Assembly guideline satisfied.
Stage 1 complete.
