BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create Holder Base Plate  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.080×0.080×0.005 m and name it holder_base.
Rotate holder_base so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor holder_base to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).

---  SECTION 2 – Create Holder Back Panel  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.080×0.120×0.002 m and name it holder_back.
Rotate holder_back so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align holder_back.bottom_face to holder_base.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.002).

---  SECTION 3 – Create Holder Left Wall  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.002×0.120×0.080 m and name it holder_left.
Rotate holder_left so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align holder_left.right_face to holder_back.left_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).

---  SECTION 4 – Create Holder Right Wall  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.002×0.120×0.080 m and name it holder_right.
Rotate holder_right so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align holder_right.left_face to holder_back.right_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).

---  SECTION 5 – Create Holder Top Lip  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.080×0.002×0.002 m and name it holder_top.
Rotate holder_top so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align holder_top.bottom_face to holder_back.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).

---  SECTION 6 – Assemble Holder Union  ---
Boolean-union holder_base, holder_back, holder_left, holder_right, and holder_top; keep holder_base.
Rename the resulting object to holder_union.
Stage 6 complete.

---  SECTION 7 – Create Yellow Note Sheet  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.076×0.076×0.0001 m and name it note_yellow.
Rotate note_yellow so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align note_yellow.bottom_face to holder_base.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.005).

---  SECTION 8 – Create Pink Note Sheet  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.076×0.076×0.0001 m and name it note_pink.
Rotate note_pink so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align note_pink.bottom_face to note_yellow.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.0001).

---  SECTION 9 – Create Blue Note Sheet  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.076×0.076×0.0001 m and name it note_blue.
Rotate note_blue so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align note_blue.bottom_face to note_pink.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.0001).

---  SECTION 10 – Create Green Note Sheet  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.076×0.076×0.0001 m and name it note_green.
Rotate note_green so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align note_green.bottom_face to note_blue.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.0001).

---  SECTION 11 – Create Orange Note Sheet  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.076×0.076×0.0001 m and name it note_orange.
Rotate note_orange so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align note_orange.bottom_face to note_green.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.0001).

---  SECTION 12 – Assemble Note Stack  ---
Boolean-union note_yellow, note_pink, note_blue, note_green, and note_orange; keep note_yellow.
Rename the resulting object to notes_stack.
Stage 12 complete.

---  SECTION 13 – Final Assembly  ---
Boolean-union holder_union and notes_stack; keep holder_union.
Rename the resulting object to final_product.
Stage 13 complete.
