────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

Define material copper_wire; diffuse_color = (0.87,0.27,0.09,1.0).
Define material magnetic_steel; diffuse_color = (0.4,0.4,0.45,1.0).

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create Stator Frame: Outer Housing  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.200 m and height 0.150 m and name it outer_housing.
Rotate outer_housing so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move outer_housing to pos=(0,0,0).
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create Stator Frame: Inner Core Ring  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.180 m and height 0.140 m and name it inner_core_ring.
Rotate inner_core_ring so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align inner_core_ring.bottom_face to outer_housing.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.005).
Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Create Stator Frame: Winding Slots  ---
Create an Empty helper (cube display) and name it winding_slots.
Rotate winding_slots so its axis aligns +Z (world).
Align winding_slots.center to outer_housing.center.
Align winding_slots.bottom_face to outer_housing.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.005).

For i in 0…3:
  Create primitive cuboid with size 0.010×0.010×0.140 m and name it slot_cut_i.
  Rotate slot_cut_i so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
  Anchor slot_cut_i to winding_slots.
  Then position at polar(i*90°; dr=0.085).
  Boolean-subtract slot_cut_i from outer_housing; discard slot_cut_i.

Stage 3 complete.

---  SECTION 4 – Create Rotor Core: Core Body  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.100 m and height 0.120 m and name it core_body.
Rotate core_body so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Move core_body to pos=(0,0,0.075).
Stage 4 complete.

---  SECTION 5 – Create Rotor Core: Magnets  ---
Create an Empty helper (cube display) and name it magnets.
Rotate magnets so its axis aligns +Z (world).
Align magnets.center to core_body.center.
Align magnets.bottom_face to core_body.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.001).

For i in 0…3:
  Create primitive cuboid with size 0.020×0.020×0.010 m and name it magnet_i.
  Rotate magnet_i so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
  Anchor magnet_i to magnets.
  Then position at polar(i*90°; dr=0.045).
  Boolean-union magnet_i with core_body; discard magnet_i.

Stage 5 complete.

---  SECTION 6 – Create Rotor Core: Windings  ---
Create an Empty helper (cube display) and name it windings.
Rotate windings so its axis aligns +Z (world).
Align windings.center to core_body.center.
Align windings.bottom_face to core_body.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.011).

For i in 0…3:
  Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.015 m and height 0.020 m and name it coil_i.
  Rotate coil_i so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
  Anchor coil_i to windings.
  Then position at polar(i*90°; dr=0.045).
  Boolean-union coil_i with core_body; discard coil_i.

Stage 6 complete.

---  SECTION 7 – Create Shaft  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.020 m and height 0.200 m and name it shaft.
Rotate shaft so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align shaft.center to generator_assembly.center.
Align shaft.bottom_face to stator_frame.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,-0.025).
Stage 7 complete.

---  SECTION 8 – Assemble Rotor Core to Shaft  ---
Align rotor_core.center to shaft.center.
Align rotor_core.bottom_face to shaft.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.060).
Boolean-union rotor_core with shaft; discard rotor_core.
Stage 8 complete.

---  SECTION 9 – Create End Cover Top  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.180 m and height 0.010 m and name it end_cover_top.
Rotate end_cover_top so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align end_cover_top.center to generator_assembly.center.
Align end_cover_top.bottom_face to stator_frame.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.005).
Stage 9 complete.

---  SECTION 10 – Create End Cover Bottom  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.180 m and height 0.010 m and name it end_cover_bottom.
Rotate end_cover_bottom so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align end_cover_bottom.center to generator_assembly.center.
Align end_cover_bottom.top_face to stator_frame.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,-0.005).
