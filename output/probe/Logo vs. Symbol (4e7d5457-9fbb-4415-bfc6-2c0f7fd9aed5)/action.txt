BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material mat_black; diffuse_color = (0.05,0.05,0.05,1.0).
Define material mat_white; diffuse_color = (0.95,0.95,0.95,1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create base_plate  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.400 m and height 0.002 m and name it base_plate.
Rotate base_plate so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align base_plate.XY_face to (0,0).
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create graphic_element  ---
Create primitive cube with size 0.080×0.060×0.005 m and name it graphic_element.
Rotate graphic_element so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align graphic_element.XY_face to (-0.10, 0.02).
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Create text_block  ---
Create primitive cube with size 0.120×0.020×0.005 m and name it text_block.
Rotate text_block so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align text_block.XY_face to (-0.10, -0.04).
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 3 complete.

---  SECTION 4 – Create icon_base  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.100 m and height 0.005 m and name it icon_base.
Rotate icon_base so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align icon_base.XY_face to (0.10, 0.02).
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 4 complete.

---  SECTION 5 – Create icon_core  ---
Create primitive cube with size 0.040×0.040×0.005 m and name it icon_core.
Rotate icon_core so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align icon_core.XY_face to (0.10, 0.02).
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 5 complete.

---  SECTION 6 – Assemble logo_group  ---
Boolean-union graphic_element and text_block; keep graphic_element.
Stage 6 complete.

---  SECTION 7 – Assemble symbol_group  ---
Boolean-union icon_core and icon_base; keep icon_base.
Stage 7 complete.

---  SECTION 8 – Assemble root  ---
Boolean-union base_plate, logo_group, and symbol_group; keep base_plate.
Stage 8 complete.

All stages complete.
