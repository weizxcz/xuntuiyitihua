BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material metal_silver; diffuse_color = (0.76,0.76,0.76,1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create outer ring  ---
Create primitive torus with Outer Diameter 0.200 m and Tube Radius 0.005 m and name it outer_ring.
Rotate outer_ring so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align outer_ring.center to peace_symbol.center.
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create vertical stem  ---
Create primitive cylinder with Diameter 0.010 m and Height 0.100 m and name it vertical_stem.
Rotate vertical_stem so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align vertical_stem.center to outer_ring.center.
Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Create left diagonal  ---
Create primitive cylinder with Diameter 0.010 m and Length 0.0707 m and name it left_diagonal.
Rotate left_diagonal so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align left_diagonal.center to outer_ring.center.
Then rotate left_diagonal by axis:Z,45°.
Stage 3 complete.

---  SECTION 4 – Create right diagonal  ---
Create primitive cylinder with Diameter 0.010 m and Length 0.0707 m and name it right_diagonal.
Rotate right_diagonal so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align right_diagonal.center to outer_ring.center.
Then rotate right_diagonal by axis:Z,-45°.
Stage 4 complete.

---  SECTION 5 – Assemble peace symbol  ---
Boolean-union outer_ring, vertical_stem, left_diagonal, and right_diagonal to form peace_symbol.
Assembly guideline satisfied.
All stages complete.
