BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material ceramic_white; diffuse_color = (0.95,0.95,0.95,1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create base plate  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.203 m and height 0.005 m and name it base_plate.
Rotate base_plate so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor base_plate to bowl_root.center.
Align base_plate.+Z_face to bowl_root.base_plate.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.001).
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create bowl shell  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.203 m and height 0.076 m and name it bowl_shell.
Rotate bowl_shell so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor bowl_shell to bowl_root.center.
Align bowl_shell.center to bowl_root.center.
Align bowl_shell.bottom_face to bowl_root.base_plate.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.001).
Stage 2 complete.

All stages complete.
