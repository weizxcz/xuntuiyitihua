BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create Body Shell  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.30 m and height 0.12 m and name it body_shell.
Rotate body_shell so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align body_shell.center to guitar_root.center.
Then move by offset(0,0,-0.06).

---  SECTION 2 – Create Soundhole Cutter  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.05 m and height 0.13 m and name it soundhole_cutter.
Rotate soundhole_cutter so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align soundhole_cutter.center to body_shell.center.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Boolean-subtract soundhole_cutter from body_shell; discard soundhole_cutter.

---  SECTION 3 – Create Bridge Block  ---
Create primitive cube with size 0.08×0.02×0.01 m and name it bridge_block.
Rotate bridge_block so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align bridge_block.center to body_shell.bottom_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.06).

---  SECTION 4 – Create Neck Body  ---
Create primitive cube with size 0.05×0.70×0.04 m and name it neck_body.
Rotate neck_body so its +Z axis aligns +Y (world).
Align neck_body.center to body_shell.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.06).

---  SECTION 5 – Create Headstock  ---
Create primitive cube with size 0.06×0.10×0.03 m and name it headstock.
Rotate headstock so its +Z axis aligns +Y (world).
Align headstock.center to neck_body.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.35).

---  SECTION 6 – Create Nut Block  ---
Create primitive cube with size 0.05×0.005×0.005 m and name it nut_block.
Rotate nut_block so its +Z axis aligns +Y (world).
Align nut_block.center to neck_body.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.35).

---  SECTION 7 – Create Tuning Pegs  ---
For r in 0…1 and c in 0…2:
  Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.01 m and height 0.03 m and name it tuning_pegs_r_c.
  Rotate tuning_pegs_r_c so its +Z axis aligns +X (world).
  Align tuning_pegs_r_c.center to headstock.left_face.
  Then move by offset(0,0,0.025 + c*0.015 + r*0.01).

---  SECTION 8 – Create Strings  ---
For c in 0…5:
  Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.0005 m and height 0.75 m and name it strings_c.
  Rotate strings_c so its +Z axis aligns +Y (world).
  Align strings_c.center to nut_block.center.
  Then move by offset(-0.02 + c*0.008, 0, 0).
