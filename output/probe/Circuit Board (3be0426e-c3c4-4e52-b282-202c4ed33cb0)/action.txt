BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create base PCB substrate  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.100×0.080×0.002 m and name it pcb_base.
Rotate pcb_base so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor pcb_base to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create copper trace layer  ---

Stage 2 complete.
