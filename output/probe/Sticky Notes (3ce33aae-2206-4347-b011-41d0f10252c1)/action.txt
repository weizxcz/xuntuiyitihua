BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create holder base  ---
Create primitive cylinder with diameter 0.100 m and height 0.015 m and name it holder_base.
Rotate holder_base so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor holder_base to world origin.
Then move by offset(0,0,0).
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create holder back  ---
Create primitive cuboid with size 0.100×0.080×0.015 m and name it holder_back.
Rotate holder_back so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Align holder_back.bottom_face to holder_base.top_face.
Then move by offset(0,0,0.0075).
Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Create yellow note stack  ---
