BLOCK 0 – Scene Reset & Units
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Delete every existing object to start from a clean scene.
Set the length unit to **metres**.

---

BLOCK 1 – Materials
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Define material fabric_nylon; diffuse_color = (0.8, 0.8, 0.8, 1.0).
Define material foam_memory; diffuse_color = (0.1, 0.1, 0.1, 1.0).

---

BLOCK 2 – Stage-by-Stage Operations
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

---  SECTION 1 – Create foam_core structure  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.04 m and length 0.15 m and name it left_arm.
Rotate left_arm so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor left_arm to world origin.
Then move by offset(-0.08, 0, 0).

Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.04 m and length 0.15 m and name it right_arm.
Rotate right_arm so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor right_arm to world origin.
Then move by offset(0.08, 0, 0).

Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.04 m and length 0.16 m and name it back_connector.
Rotate back_connector so its +Z axis aligns +X (world).
Anchor back_connector to world origin.
Then move by offset(0, 0, 0).

Boolean-union left_arm, right_arm, and back_connector to form foam_core.
Stage 1 complete.

---  SECTION 2 – Create fabric_cover structure  ---
Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.045 m and length 0.15 m and name it left_sleeve.
Rotate left_sleeve so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor left_sleeve to world origin.
Then move by offset(-0.08, 0, 0).

Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.045 m and length 0.15 m and name it right_sleeve.
Rotate right_sleeve so its +Z axis aligns +Z (world).
Anchor right_sleeve to world origin.
Then move by offset(0.08, 0, 0).

Create primitive cylinder with radius 0.045 m and length 0.16 m and name it back_sleeve.
Rotate back_sleeve so its +Z axis aligns +X (world).
Anchor back_sleeve to world origin.
Then move by offset(0, 0, 0).

Boolean-union left_sleeve, right_sleeve, and back_sleeve to form fabric_cover.
Stage 2 complete.

---  SECTION 3 – Assemble travel_pillow  ---
Boolean-union foam_core and fabric_cover to form travel_pillow.
Stage 3 complete.

All stages complete.
