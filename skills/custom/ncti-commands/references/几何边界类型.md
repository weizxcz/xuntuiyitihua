## 提取边或面的UV值

**类型：** 基础命令

**说明：** 根据实例化的文档对象中及几何实体对象名称，提取边或面的UV值

**示例：**

```python
doc.RunCommand("cmd_ncti_create_ellipse", "e1", 5, 3)
doc.RunCommand("cmd_ncti_create_plane", "p1", "e1")
doc.RunCommand("cmd_ncti_delete", ["e1"])
geom = NCTI.BodyBounds(doc,"p1")
print([geom.BoundU1,geom.BoundU2,geom.BoundV1,geom.BoundV2])
```
